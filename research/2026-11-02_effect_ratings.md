## Why the whole-chain estimate rises

The [October 31 board](2026-10-31_75_goal_report.md) carried the chain and grade
at 75 while focused improvements accumulated. The earlier
[highlight cooperation](2026-10-31_highlight_cooperation.md) retained about 9%
more selected white-core separation; [weak-material color preservation](2026-10-31_weak_material_colors.md)
added useful lower-tone color distinctions with documented costs. The user then
adopted BLACKS=0.0025 in both profiles. The [Arc trial](2026-11-01_arc_black_trial.md)
supports its Arc material benefit, not an equivalent GZW appearance conclusion.

The [shared tonal base](2026-11-02_tonal_architecture.md) now addresses a repeated
cooperation problem. Selected local shade response increases about 2.2% for
bedroom cloth, 4.3% for plaster, 3.8% for floor and 3.3% for the interior cool
window. Both games contribute supporting material observations. All 18 selected
flat-face median edge L/C gaps are zero. Current white-core separation is
5.8347→5.8589, preserving the preceding light treatment while improving materials.

For this requested reassessment, source/previous/selected overviews of all seven
current cases were reviewed again: Arc interior, bedroom, daylight and emitters;
GZW daylight and dark; and vivid Arc content using the GZW profile. Native material
crops and the existing attribution reports provide the supporting context.
This is a qualitative synthesis of the final chain's behavior and retained
tradeoffs. It is not a new all-effect bypass campaign, a conversion of percentage
gains into rating points, or an average of the individual rows.

The chain now earns a modest increase for improving useful material relationships
while retaining the palette/light work. The same evidence supports 78 for grade
overall and a scoped 80 for the joint tonal model. It does not independently
raise Retinex, zone contrast, lift, print or inverse scores.

## Why 80 is not yet a confident whole-chain estimate

- Arc road local response rises 0.9901→1.0409; GZW paving already had expanded
  contrast and receives more. Greater texture response is not always preferable.
- Small vehicle, dark-metal/shelf, cloud and halo costs remain. Several individual
  appearance blocks still have borderline 75 estimates.
- The source reconstruction assumptions and some palette/hue choices remain
  uncertain. The new brightness estimate does not identify artistic intent.
- Chart gradient steps grow slightly. GPU cost at actual game rates is unprofiled;
  zero drops in private 30 fps captures do not establish that cost.
- Coverage remains two games/profiles and selected lighting. Cached motion has
  15 unique poses/sec doubled to 30; dense native movement, physical high refresh,
  further games/resolutions/aspects and broad preference remain unassessed.

These limits qualify the ratings without creating a new mandatory repair or
parameter-search queue. The long-term goal remains a more consistently useful
whole-chain 80, with the 75+ acceptance definition unchanged.

baseline pairs, two numerical GPU readbacks, exact tonal-zero controls, both
