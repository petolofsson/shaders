# Bounded shadow-color numeric and ramp checks

The provisional [bounded candidate](2026-11-06_shadow_color_bounded_screen.md) was tested without changing production, profiles or configs. Its exact offset body was copied into an isolated RGBA32F diagnostic and read back with a 24-bit encoded screenshot. A separate full-chain chart covers neutral, warm, cool, mixed and neutral-to-warm 8-bit source ramps, plus a deterministic one-code red perturbation. These checks do not establish a visible combined color-and-brightness improvement or authorize adoption.

## Compiled arithmetic

The direct probe exercises 256 constructed RGB inputs including black, gray, primaries, mixed colors, a saturated face and near-black values. For the actual `BLACKS=0.0025` and `WHITES=0.995` shared by both profiles, maximum RGB difference against an independent double-precision CPU calculation is 0.000000330; input, affine base and BLACKS=0 discrepancies are below 0.000000295. Maximum measured luma difference between the candidate and affine base is 0.000000383, and gray outputs are identical at the readback resolution. BLACKS=0 means `RGB * 0.995` here, not identity. One of the 256 inputs activates the upper-headroom multiplier. Output readback spans approximately 0.000000119 to 1.000000179; the encoded readback step is approximately 0.000000477, so these endpoint deviations are within its quantization and are not evidence of GPU overrange. This finite test is not an exhaustive GPU gamut or continuity proof.

The first diagnostic used `WHITES=1` rather than the actual profile value; it is preserved separately in `numeric_bounded_report.json`. The corrected profile-value result is `numeric_profile_report.json`. Both diagnostic runs had zero reported dropped/delayed frames and preserved production hashes. The probe copies the actual candidate statements, with only their inputs and profile constants parameterized; it does not test downstream gamut projection or same-update adaptive state.

An independent double-precision sweep of 20,001 points from 0 to 0.02 along each neutral, warm, cool, red and blue linear-RGB ray finds positive luma steps and maximum luma difference from the affine base below 1.7e-18. Saturated red and blue rays include decreasing non-dominant channels as the zero-luma correction grows. This is an isolated sampled arithmetic observation, not a full-chain or GPU continuity guarantee. Results are in `continuity_cpu.json`.

## Full-chain chart

For each profile, current and candidate each have two separately settled captures. All eight private headless processes report zero dropped/delayed frames. Actual normalized shader bodies match current production or exactly the intended candidate, input clip and captured-file hashes verify, and before/after production hashes match. The 20-second still protocol does **not** yield exact repeats: current/candidate maximum variation is one code, affecting 1,585,571/17 pixels in GZW and 12,069/39,369 pixels in Arc, with the marker masked. Therefore small between-variant changes cannot be assigned confidently to the candidate or interpreted as convergence.

For the five 56-step source ramps, mean output linear luminance increases at every source step in both profiles and variants. The one-code perturbation has positive mean output luminance difference at all 56 levels in both variants and profiles. These are averages over artificial flat strips, not pixelwise monotonicity, spatial-edge, color-order or temporal guarantees. Candidate-versus-current differences reach three 8-bit codes; no additional all-channel-black pixels were counted. Native results and full metrics are under `captures/shadow_color_bounded_20261106/` (`chart_report.json`, `chart_metrics.json` and PNGs). The source is synthetic; it does not replace real warm/red-black examples, noise characterization or measured same-update motion.

The tests support the sampled arithmetic but do not resolve the existing neutral-source shifts, source-relative reversals or missing brightness-gradient benefit. Future tests should preserve the chart repeat failure and uncertainty; do not assert the isolated ramp results establish whole-chain continuity. The [test list](2026-11-06_shadow_color_todo.md) retains the outstanding decision work.
