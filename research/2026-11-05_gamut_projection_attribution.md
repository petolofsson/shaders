# Final gamut projection attribution — 2026-11-05

**Decision: do not pursue a gamut-mapping candidate on this evidence.** The final grade projection's changes are spatially sparse in the two tested scenes and do not identify a clear large visual improvement. This is not a universal gamut acceptance claim. No production shader, profile, config or rating changed.

Two explicit diagnostics use isolated copies of the current combined pipeline and profile: the Arc vivid daylight source processed with the current GZW profile, and the Arc emitter scene with the Arc profile. These are two profiles but one source game, not cross-game coverage. The probe intercepts the `result` immediately before the final `OklabToSDR(RGBtoOklabLinear(result))` in `ColorTransformPS`, after grade chroma and before look/print. It compares that input with the projected result on the same GPU update. This does not measure inverse-stage gamut projection, later print/encoding, other frames, or actual game-rate behavior.

A full-resolution diagnostic output encodes masks per source pixel: red where any input channel is below -0.001, green where any channel is above 1.001, blue where any channel changes by at least 0.01 linear RGB after projection. The two leftmost/topmost marker pixels in each direction are excluded. Of 3,686,396 assessed pixels, vivid has 393 below the lower threshold, none above the upper threshold, and 17 with a >=0.01 channel change. Emitter has 481, zero and 24 respectively. Both independently captured mask repeats are pixel-identical. The >0.01 vivid pixels cluster on the orange sleeve and the emitter pixels on the red lamp edge. Native source/full crops are `gzw_vivid_affected.png` and `arc_emitters_affected.png` in the local capture directory. Source-to-full images do not isolate projection appearance; the masks identify its footprint.

A second explicit readback samples one point in each 16×8 tile (28,799 samples after excluding the marker tile). It encodes six pre/post linear RGB components into sixteen-bit binary fields over [-0.5,1.5]; quantization is approximately 0.0000305 per channel. One vivid sample and four emitter samples are more than 0.001 outside SDR; maximum sampled input excursions are approximately 0.00271 and 0.00222, and the greatest sampled pre/post channel differences are approximately 0.00681 and 0.00678. No encoded component hit the encoding limits; both encoded readback pairs match. Grid sampling may miss fine features and its tiled UV remapping is diagnostic; the independent full-resolution mask supplies the footprint result. Neither probe compares repeated source-update ages in motion.

All eight completed private headless processes reported zero dropped/delayed frames. The first readback build failed due to unreachable code left by the diagnostic injection; the failure is retained in `/tmp/shader-runtime-_2xxxlro/vkbasalt.log` and was corrected in the isolated generator. Subsequent probe and mask config/file hashes were verified, and source hashes before/after match. Static 20-second settling and exact repeats are not proof of arbitrary timing or dense gameplay stability. Data, scripts, masks, and readback reports are ignored local artifacts in `captures/gamut_readback_20261105/`.

The earlier blue-cusp repair and its known extreme-blue and gradient costs are not invalidated by these observations. Broader saturated games, input conditions, per-stage measurement, charts and physical high-refresh motion could reveal other behavior, but this finite screen provides no basis for a new mapping or parameter sweep.
