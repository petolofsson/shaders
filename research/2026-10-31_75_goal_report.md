## What 75 means

Under [GOAL.md](../GOAL.md), 75 means a repeatable, useful contribution to fuller
SDR color relationships, nuanced shadows and convincing light, with unobtrusive
behavior, acceptable tradeoffs and restraint when intervention is unnecessary.
It does not mean 75% accuracy, recovered HDR information or 75% completion.

Appearance estimates have roughly 5–10 point uncertainty. A 75 entry is
borderline. Correctness evidence supports the judgment but does not establish
visual effectiveness. Foundation blocks are judged in their measurement/signal
roles; creative blocks are judged by their contribution as used. Parent/child
rows overlap: do not average scores or calculate a pass percentage from them.

## What the three follow-ups achieved

**Print/look — 70→75, borderline.** The shadow-protected RGB-ratio path retains
the helper's luminance curve and improves selected sky hue preservation in both
profiles. The source-selected shelf is pixel-identical to baseline, and the
prior small-light separation improvement remains. Warm-floor and red-lamp color
variation improve. Blue-wall/cool-window color separation, stronger synthetic
primaries, warm-hue and quantization costs remain acceptable on the reviewed set.

**Three-way CC — 70→75, borderline.** Half-strength shadow temperature/tint
restored more shelf warmth/variation and reduced selected foliage source-relative
hue shifts from about 11–12° to 4.5–4.8°, retaining cool neutral shadows and
selected density/light cores. Palette, one-code near-black and startup costs
remain. This is a scoped preset-cooperation judgment, not universal acceptance.

**Inverse chroma — 70→75, borderline.** The existing hue budget now respects
available SDR gamut at the pixel's lightness. It abstains at unsafe boundaries
rather than adding chroma that the final projection must undo. In the frozen GPU
probe, maximum colored-input hue change falls about 1.49°→0.00057°, while useful
expansion remains active. Final bypasses show modest selected sky/lamp richness
without affecting white light cores. This adds observable restraint beyond the
previous hue-only budget. It still cannot infer arbitrary artistic intent or
prove that source chroma was compressed; the score reflects its bounded observed
contribution, not successful recovery of unknown original scene data.
