# Output-chroma preservation proxy

**Decision: reject this version.** A direct expansion of the current output's linear-RGB chroma preserves exact gray and ideal luminance and gives attractive selected shelf/metal measurements, but it amplifies existing color casts and makes larger changes to saturated content, including an Arc HUD element. A headroom cap avoids out-of-range intermediate RGB in the tested screenshots but does not prevent substantial hue/channel changes. This is an offline 8-bit proxy, not a production shader or a universal judgment of output chroma expansion.

## Model

Use the current output RGB `v` and its linear luminance `y` to form `delta=0.15*gate*(v-y)`, where the gate fades at near-zero diagnostic grade-input luminance and above output Y=0.01–0.08. For neutral RGB, the correction is exactly zero in ideal arithmetic. Its linear luminance is zero because `Luma(v-y)=0`. It scales the current RGB departure from neutral without copying the input's color, so it cannot distinguish intentional shadow tint from material color; it may strengthen both. The coefficient is one exploratory value, not a recommended user control or a strength sweep.

The first proxy clipped the result to SDR and exposed out-of-range channels. The bounded version scales all three correction channels by a common factor limited by both lower and upper channel headroom. This retains ideal zero-luma correction for nonnegative SDR input; it cannot by itself assure Oklab hue, source palette, quantized ordering, continuity or GPU behavior. The ordered-brightness research curve then acts on the result. The inputs are separately captured 8-bit current output and diagnostic grade-input screenshots, and the final proxy is re-encoded to 8-bit. It is not a same-update full-float or compiled implementation. The prior brightness curve remains scene-fitted and has its own cool-material costs.

## Results and decision

Selected source-b-quartile color distances current→combined change 0.327→0.381 on the GZW shelf, 1.575→1.840 on Arc dark metal, 0.092→0.096 on blue wall, and 1.970→2.220 on cool window. Four-pixel source-relative reversal rates are 6.49%→6.48%, 5.75%→5.76%, 2.63%→2.80%, and 1.98%→2.09%, respectively. These overlapping, quantization-sensitive metrics describe the selected regions; larger color distance is not a quality score. Shelf and metal brightness separations also rise with the ordered curve, while selected wall/window brightness gaps fall as in the earlier proxy.

Across 5,744 and 5,138 source-neutral samples, median output Oklab a/b ×100 shifts GZW `[-0.158,-0.043]→[-0.187,-0.043]` and Arc `[0.165,-0.396]→[0.183,-0.424]`. Source-neutral selection is from a separate quantized screenshot and is not a claim that the grade input is neutral. In the complete 2560×1440 screenshots, the combined bounded proxy changes as many as 11 codes in one channel; the color-only operation also reaches 11 codes. It changes more than five codes in 9,090 GZW and 506 Arc pixels for color alone. The maximum-change examples include foliage and an Arc HUD element; native crops are in the capture directory. They are diagnostic observations, not proof that all such changes are objectionable or visible in gameplay, but they are inconsistent with adopting this unvalidated model solely from favorable dark-crop statistics. The combined proxy creates no new all-channel-black pixels; 476 GZW and 117 Arc channels become code zero, a different and weaker endpoint observation.

Bounded color math has no channel outside `[-1e-7,1+1e-7]` on these two screenshots, whereas the original unclipped version had 397 and 105 out-of-range channel values. Small floating-point roundoff around zero is not counted as an SDR violation. This is not a GPU bound proof or comprehensive gamut screen. Source/profile/config identities, full-frame controls, native target and cost crops, and both scripts are preserved under `captures/shadow_output_chroma_20261106/` and `captures/shadow_chroma_bounded_20261106/`. There are no new GPU, motion, exact-black or activation tests. Production shaders, profiles, configs and ratings remain unchanged.
