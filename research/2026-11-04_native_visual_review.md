## Scope and observations

Reviewed matched source/full outputs in overview and native 800×700 crops: GZW dark shelf, Arc trader interior, Arc red/white emitter scene, and Arc vivid daylight content processed with the current GZW profile. The last is not a third game. The vivid scene preserves strong blue, warm roof/ground and foliage relationships; the selected emitter crop retains the white and red sources. The dark shelf and interior show a darker, denser response in places; local source shading remains visible and preference for the darker look is not resolved. These are visual judgments on static screenshots, not blind tests or proof of visible degradation in motion. Full images and crops are in `captures/visible_review_20261104/`.

Two fresh private headless all-effects runs compared current full, unchanged repeat, and individual inverse-luma, BLACKS/WHITES and complete tonal-gate bypasses in the two dark scenes. Five fresh processes per scene, 20 s continuous settling, 5 s compile delay. Both runs report unchanged full/repeat mean ΔE 0.000; this is not a claim about convergence at all times. Current source hashes in their reports match production on review. The all-effects tool sets diagnostic stage gates to 100; no profile or shader was intentionally changed. Existing same-profile captures supply print-stock and chroma-gate bypass comparisons; their full/repeat reports also give zero mean ΔE. Each bypass is a single capture, not a repeated pair. Raw source PNG comparison is not an independent validated transfer-calibration capture.

The print bypass visibly lightens the dark crops, but removes print density and changes the look. Black/white mapping and the tonal gate also affect the dark response; inverse-luma is less influential at whole-frame scale. In GZW the all-effects report gives whole-frame contribution ΔE 0.08 inverse luma, 1.28 black/white and 2.45 tonal; Arc gives 0.18, 1.62 and 1.28. These numbers measure effect removal across the full image, not shadow-detail quality, and bypassing multiple interconnected adjustments cannot attribute unique causality. The print bypass was captured independently. Existing configured BLACKS is a deliberate user choice and remains unchanged.

The ten new captures succeeded; elapsed settling and screenshot repeat do not prove dense-motion stability or GPU state. The tool reports runtime logs under `/tmp/shader-runtime-*`; no numerical state readback, candidate or subjective user review was performed. Source and profile/config epochs must match before future reuse. No shader, profile or rating change follows. Next useful evidence would be broader representative in-game captures where users can identify a plainly objectionable recurring loss, rather than another numerical or parameter screen.
