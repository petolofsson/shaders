# Restrained shadow-color screen

The user clarified that shadow color should be very subtle: the image should feel more dimensional without a noticeable tint or obvious treatment. This screen tests one isolated half-strength version of the [bounded black-offset color candidate](2026-11-06_shadow_color_bounded_screen.md). It multiplies its already headroom-limited RGB correction by 0.5; it does not alter the user's BLACKS value. The test uses existing GZW dark and Arc interior sources, current profiles and separate 20-second full/repeat captures.

**Decision: do not adopt or proceed to a strength sweep on this evidence.** At native crop size the half-strength change is unobtrusive and selected color distances fall between current and the earlier candidate, but the visual benefit is difficult to establish. Some source-neutral shifts remain and selected shade-ordering reversals get worse, even compared with the full candidate. Brightness separation barely changes. These results do not establish that the candidate is objectionable in gameplay, or that the reversals are necessarily perceptible; they do not justify an automatic production change for the requested subtle effect either. Continue the separate brightness-gradient investigation from a concrete mechanism; keep this color idea provisional rather than assuming larger color distance means a better image.
